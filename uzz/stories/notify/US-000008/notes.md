# Notes — notify/US-000008

* Cut agreed in the sound review: this story is the playback (bundled sounds, volume, fallback, one sound for rapid notifications, stop on dismiss/snooze); choosing the sound, the volume slider, a custom file and the preview are settings/US-000016. WAV and AIFF only (JavaFX `AudioClip`); MP3 would need `MediaPlayer` and is a story of its own.
* The four sounds are original and synthesized by `tools/make_sounds.py` (sine partials with exponential decay, 44.1 kHz mono 16-bit, peak 0.8): no licence question. They are plain tones: replace the WAVs in `src/main/resources/ste/wakemeapp/notify/sounds/` with anything better (same names), or tweak the script and run it again.
* `SoundNotifier` holds the rules and is tested with a fake `SoundOutput`; `AudioClipOutput` is the one JavaFX class (clips are cached per sound, so the second play has no load time). Settings are read at every notification, so a change in the dialog counts at once.
* "Only one sound for two notifications within a second" uses the clock of the notifier; the overlay of the second still shows (the manager shows one meeting at a time, as before; merging meetings in one overlay is not in this story, the scenario's "queues or merges" is left to the overlay stories).
* Played from `CLILauncher.showOverlay`, not from `OverlayManager.showNotification`, because the manager also shows the overlay again on "undo dismiss", which must stay silent. The test notification takes the same `sound::play` as a hook.
* The overlay of a notification of the tray application is created per notification as before; dismissing it calls `sound.stop()` first. A dismissal made while no poller exists (`--now`) is also handled now (the listener is always set).
* Not verified here: `AudioClipOutput`, the launcher and `TestNotificationTest` need `mvn test` and a listen on a desktop (the real sound, the volume, the stop); `SoundNotifierTest`, `SoundSettingTest` and `ConfigReportTest` were run.
