## notify/US-000008 — Configurable Sound Notification

### User Story

```
AS A User
I WANT a sound to play when a meeting notification appears
SO THAT I am alerted even if I'm not looking at the screen
```

#### Status
DONE

#### Dependencies
- notify/US-000005
- settings/US-000017

#### Technical Context & Constraints
* **Target System**: JavaFX desktop app (all platforms; macOS primary)
* **Toolchain / Rules**: Maven + JavaFX 25 + Java 25
* **Audio API**: JavaFX `AudioClip` (WAV/AIFF, no delay at start); a custom file and MP3 are not in this story (settings/US-000016, WAV and AIFF only)
* **Bundled Sounds**: 4 short WAVs (chime, pop, ping, bell), synthesized by `tools/make_sounds.py`, in `ste/wakemeapp/notify/sounds/`; default: chime
* **Volume**: stored 0-100 % (default 80), independent of the system volume; chosen in settings/US-000016
* **Fallback**: system beep if the sound cannot be played; the reason is logged, not shown
* **Playback**: non-blocking, plays once

#### Notes
1. Notifications less than a second apart share one sound; the second is not queued or merged, its overlay is shown as usual (the overlay shows one meeting at a time)
2. The sound is loaded at start (`SoundNotifier.prepare`) so that the first one has no delay
3. It plays where the overlay is shown (`CLILauncher.showOverlay`, so a snoozed meeting plays again) and for the test notification; not for "undo dismiss", which only shows the overlay again
4. Dismissing or snoozing stops it
5. Reminders switched off (settings/US-000017) never reach it: nothing is shown, so nothing plays. The log line is "Notification suppressed (globally disabled)"
6. A volume of 0 is silent on purpose: no beep

### Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Scenario: Default sound plays with notification
  Given the user has not customized the notification sound
  And notifications are enabled
  When a meeting notification is triggered
  Then the bundled chime plays once at 80 % volume
  And the overlay appears simultaneously

Scenario: The chosen bundled sound plays
  Given the stored sound is "bell"
  When a meeting notification is triggered
  Then the bell plays once

Scenario: Sound volume is configurable
  Given the stored volume is 50
  When a notification triggers
  Then the sound plays at half the maximum amplitude
  And system volume is unaffected

Scenario: Volume zero is silent
  Given the stored volume is 0
  When a notification triggers
  Then no sound plays and no beep either
  And the overlay appears

Scenario: Fallback to system beep on audio failure
  Given the sound cannot be loaded or played
  When a notification triggers
  Then the system beep plays
  And the overlay still appears
  And a warning is logged (not shown to user)

Scenario: Sound respects notification enable/disable
  Given notifications are disabled in settings
  When a meeting notification would trigger
  Then no sound plays
  And no overlay appears

Scenario: Multiple rapid notifications don't overlap sounds
  Given two meetings trigger less than 1 second apart
  When the notifications are processed
  Then only one sound plays (the first)
  And both overlays are shown

Scenario: Dismissing or snoozing stops the sound
  Given the sound is playing
  When the user dismisses or snoozes the notification
  Then the sound stops

Scenario: The test notification plays the sound
  When the user clicks "Test Notification"
  Then the sound plays with the test overlay
```
